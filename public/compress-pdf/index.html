Need a lighter PDF for sharing or storage? ARNZO's current compression tool renders each page as an image and rebuilds the document using JPEG compression. Choose a quality level to balance appearance and file size.

How to compress pdf online

  1. Choose Compress PDF and select one PDF file.
  2. Select lower, balanced or higher image quality depending on your needs.
  3. Process the document. Your browser renders pages and rebuilds them as JPEG-based PDF pages.
  4. Download the result and compare its size and readability with the original.

Important details and limitations

ARNZO PDF Toolkit runs document operations in your browser. PDF contents are not uploaded to ARNZO servers for processing. Processing time and practical file size depend on your browser, device memory and document complexity. Keep an untouched copy of important documents before making changes.

Compression is image-based: searchable text, links, forms and vector quality are not preserved, and the resulting file is not guaranteed to be smaller.

Frequently asked questions

Will compression always make my PDF smaller?

No. Image-based rebuilding can sometimes produce a larger file, depending on the source document and selected quality.

Will searchable text be preserved?

No. The current method converts pages into JPEG images, so searchable text, links, forms and vector quality are not preserved.

Which quality should I choose?

Balanced is a reasonable starting point. Choose lower quality for smaller images or higher quality when visual detail matters more.

Are my documents uploaded?

No. Rendering and PDF creation happen locally in your browser.
