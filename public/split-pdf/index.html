Separate a large PDF into individual page files when you only need selected sections. ARNZO's Split PDF tool exports each selected page as its own PDF and bundles the results into one ZIP download.

How to split pdf online

  1. Choose the Split PDF tool and select one PDF from your device.
  2. Enter page numbers or ranges, for example 1, 3-5, 8.
  3. Check that the page numbers are within the document's page count.
  4. Process the PDF and download the ZIP containing one PDF per selected page.

Important details and limitations

ARNZO PDF Toolkit runs document operations in your browser. PDF contents are not uploaded to ARNZO servers for processing. Processing time and practical file size depend on your browser, device memory and document complexity. Keep an untouched copy of important documents before making changes.

The tool creates a new output file and does not intentionally overwrite your original document. Some advanced PDF features may not be preserved identically by every PDF library.

Frequently asked questions

How do I enter page ranges?

Use commas between pages and ranges, such as 1, 3-5, 8. Page numbering starts at 1.

What will I download?

A ZIP archive containing a separate PDF for each selected page.

Does splitting upload my document?

No. The PDF is processed locally in your browser.

Can I split a password-protected PDF?

Password-protected files may not be supported. Use an unlocked PDF if the browser cannot open your document.
