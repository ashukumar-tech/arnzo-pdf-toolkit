Rotate PDF Online

Fix pages that appear sideways or upside down. ARNZO's Rotate PDF tool changes the page rotation of your document and lets you download a corrected PDF directly to your device.

Open Rotate PDF Online →

How to rotate pdf online

  1. Select Rotate PDF and choose one PDF file.
  2. Choose 90°, 180° or 270° clockwise rotation.
  3. Click Process PDF to update the page orientation.
  4. Download the rotated PDF and check the affected pages in a PDF reader.

When is this PDF tool useful?

Everyday documents

Correct scanned pages saved in landscape or upside-down orientation.

Work and study

Make a document easier to read on screen or when printed.

Sharing files

Fix page orientation before sharing a report or form.

Important details and limitations

ARNZO PDF Toolkit runs document operations in your browser. PDF contents are not uploaded to ARNZO servers for processing. Processing time and practical file size depend on your browser, device memory and document complexity. Keep an untouched copy of important documents before making changes.

The tool creates a new output file and does not intentionally overwrite your original document. Some advanced PDF features may not be preserved identically by every PDF library.

Frequently asked questions

Can I rotate a PDF by 90 degrees?

Yes. Choose 90°, 180° or 270° clockwise rotation.

Does this rotate individual selected pages?

The current tool rotates all pages in the selected PDF by the chosen angle.

Will rotation change the text content?

It changes page orientation metadata rather than converting the page into an image.

Is my PDF uploaded?

No. The PDF is processed in your browser.
